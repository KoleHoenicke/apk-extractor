APK Extractor Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

APK Extractor is designed to work entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Installed-app information

APK Extractor reads the inventory of applications installed on your device so it can show the apps available for you to extract. This information is processed locally and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Exported files

When you select an app, APK Extractor copies its installed APK files to a folder that you choose through Android's system document picker. The selected folder location is stored locally so the app can return to it for later exports. You can replace that folder selection at any time. Exported files are written only to the location you choose.

Data collection and sharing

APK Extractor contains no advertising, analytics, account, tracking, or remote-service SDK. It requests no Internet permission. The developer receives no app inventory, usage, diagnostics, exported files, or other user data from the app.

Children's privacy

APK Extractor is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this location with a new effective date.
